# Arazöz Otomotiv — Stone / Sand refinement

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation. Existing Next.js site and business content retained.

## Design DNA

- Navy #162B3E: identity, hero and photographic gradients.
- Stone #B8AD9B: warm mineral services surface; Sand #D4C4A8 grounds the service area.
- Sand #D4C4A8: replaces Camel, accents and fleet ground.
- Beige #E8DFCF: quiet reading areas.
- Anton: display and navigation. Manrope: body. Two families only.
- Layout: supplied Gallery4 adapted to real fleet images; portrait cards combine photography, title, description and contact link on one surface.
- Shape: softly rounded portrait cards; vehicle occupies the upper area, legible text overlays a navy gradient below.
- Motion: Embla draggable service carousel, keyboard and arrow controls; GSAP entry choreography; three vertical rolling phrases; layered canvas water jets, droplets and drifting mist. Static reduced-motion composition.

## Brief and journey

Audience: people arranging Konya water tanker rental. Outcome: see the real fleet, find the relevant service, call or write on WhatsApp.
Real materials: original Ford Cargo hero; five authentic fleet photographs; supplied palette; confirmed Karatay address, phone, company name and existing Turkish service copy.
Tone: industrial, cinematic, readable, grounded in actual machinery.
Sequence retained: hero → company → services → fleet → rental → area → questions → contact.
Grammar: photographic gallery within an editorial page; existing navigation and ending retained.
Feeling curve: power in the hero, calm reading in the company section, clarity in services, confidence from full vehicle photographs, simple contact at the end.
Peak/signature: the wet industrial foreground comes alive with layered water spray and drifting ground mist; the visitor can increase the effect.
Tell-someone sentence: the tanker site where the water comes alive around the actual truck.
No generated vehicle images, invented statistics, testimonials or specifications.

## Scoped skill use

- frontend-design: existing local skill; explicit type/palette/layout decisions, photographed subject and deliberate visual hierarchy.
- design-dna: https://github.com/zanwei/design-dna — exact supplied palette mapped to semantic tokens, style and effect specification. Screenshot is a rejected state, not a palette to reproduce.
- design-taste-frontend: https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill — component refinement path, real images, full text, screenshot critique, reduced decoration. Existing brand and requested carousel retained.
- scrollcraft: https://github.com/nateherkai/scroll-craft — distinct foreground atmosphere, visitor-controlled effect, varied scene pacing, mobile and reduced-motion QA. No framework/engine replacement: user explicitly requested refining the existing GSAP project.

Validation uses the connected browser rather than the skills' separate browser harness, following the workspace browser-control requirement.
